Attendance FAQs

Common questions about attendance management.

Use the demo to confirm the exact workflow, device compatibility and deployment plan for your organisation.

Can GenNextOffice work with my existing biometric devices?

Yes. GenNextOffice includes device-integration workflows. Exact support depends on the device model and communication method — we'll confirm compatibility before deployment.

How does mobile attendance work for field employees?

Approved employees can use configured mobile attendance workflows, with controls such as location-based capture depending on your selected setup.

What happens when an employee forgets to punch?

Employees can submit regularisation requests through self-service. Managers review and approve them, and the attendance record is updated accordingly.

Can employees see their own attendance records?

Yes. Employee self-service provides visibility into raw punches, processed attendance, leave balances and request status.

How does attendance flow into payroll?

Once attendance is processed and approved, the pay-day values and overtime data can be pulled directly into the monthly payroll run — no re-entry.
