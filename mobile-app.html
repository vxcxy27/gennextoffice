Mobile & self-service FAQs

Common questions about employee access and mobile attendance.

Get clarity on what employees can see, how mobile attendance works, and how you keep control.

What can employees see in the self-service portal?

Employees can view their attendance records, leave balances, payslips, and request status. They can also submit leave, regularisation, OD and permission requests.

Can all employees use mobile attendance?

No — mobile attendance can be limited to employees who are authorised for the workflow. Eligibility is configurable by role, team or location.

How does location tracking work on mobile?

Location information is captured as part of approved mobile attendance workflows, with configurable accuracy and consent controls.

Can managers approve requests from mobile?

Yes. Managers can view and approve employee requests from the web portal, with notifications and reminders for pending approvals.

How do employees request leave?

Employees submit leave requests through self-service. The request is routed to the appropriate manager for approval, and the status is visible to the employee and HR.
