Payroll FAQs

Common questions about payroll management.

Get clarity on statutory rules, payroll processing, and how attendance data flows into your monthly payroll.

Does payroll automatically use attendance data?

Yes. Once attendance is processed and approved, pay-day values and overtime data can be pulled directly into the monthly payroll run — no manual re-entry.

Can we configure our own salary structures?

Yes. GenNextOffice allows you to build salary structures from earnings and deduction heads, and assign them to employees with effective dates.

How are statutory deductions handled?

You can maintain applicable payroll statutory and tax configuration — PF, PT, ESI, professional tax, and other required rules — in a controlled setup area.

Can employees access payslips?

Yes. Payslips are published to the employee self-service portal, where employees can view and download them.

What outputs are available after processing?

GenNextOffice generates payslips (PDF), payroll reports, and supported statutory export formats for operational submission workflows.
