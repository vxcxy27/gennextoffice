Common questions

A practical system, not another disconnected app.

Use the demo to confirm the exact workflow, device compatibility and deployment plan for your organisation.

Does GenNextOffice include both attendance and payroll?

Yes. The platform is designed to connect attendance operations with payroll processing, while still allowing modules and access to be controlled by requirement.

Can employees access their own information?

Yes. Employee self-service supports access to relevant attendance and payroll information plus request workflows such as leave and attendance corrections.

Can it work with biometric attendance devices?

GenNextOffice includes device-integration workflows. Exact support depends on the device model and communication method, so device compatibility should be confirmed before deployment.

Can field employees use mobile attendance?

Approved employees can use configured mobile attendance workflows, with controls such as location-based capture depending on the selected setup.

Can we see the software before buying?

Yes. Request a demo so the attendance, payroll, employee and device workflows can be shown against your actual use case.
